Before You Message

Four details that speed up your booking.

Send these with your first message and our technician brings the right parts on the first visit.

01

Brand and unit type

Wall-mounted, cassette or ceiling unit, and the brand on the front panel.

02

What it is doing

Warm air, water dripping, a noise or a smell. Tell us when it started.

03

Any error code

A blinking light or code on the display tells us which part to test first.

04

A photo of the label

The sticker on the side of the indoor unit shows the model and the gas type.
