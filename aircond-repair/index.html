Faults

Aircond problems we repair

Tell us which of these your unit is doing. It helps our technician bring the right parts on the first visit.

  • The unit runs but blows warm or room-temperature air
  • Water leaks from the indoor unit onto the wall or floor
  • The outdoor unit does not start, or clicks and stops
  • The breaker trips as soon as the unit switches on
  • A light blinks or an error code shows on the indoor unit
  • Rattling, buzzing or grinding noise while the unit runs

How We Find the Fault

We test the parts before we replace any

A unit that stops cooling has usually lost a capacitor or a fan motor rather than the compressor most people fear. We check the low-cost parts first.

Checks on every repair visit

  • Check the power supply, breaker and wiring to both units
  • Test the capacitor, fan motors and compressor with a meter
  • Read the error code and test the sensors and PCB
  • Measure the gas pressure to check for a leak
  • Replace the failed part and run the unit until it cools
