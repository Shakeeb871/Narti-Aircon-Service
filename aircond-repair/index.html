Common Faults

5 Signs Your Aircond Needs A Repair, Not Just A Service

A service cleans the unit, while a repair replaces a part that has failed. Book a repair visit if your aircond shows any of these signs.

01

Blowing Warm Air

The fan runs but the air stays warm, often a failed capacitor, fan motor or gas leak.

02

Not Turning On

No lights or no response from the remote, usually a power, PCB or sensor fault.

03

Error Code Or Blinking Light

The display blinks a code that points to a sensor, fan or communication fault.

04

Breaker Trips

The DB trips when the unit starts, a sign of a shorted compressor or wiring fault.

05

Loud Or Strange Noise

Rattling, buzzing or grinding from a loose fan, worn bearing or failing motor.
