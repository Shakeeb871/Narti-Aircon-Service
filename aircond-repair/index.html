Fault Finding

Why Your Aircond Stopped Working

These are the faults we see most in Johor homes and offices, what usually causes them, and how we repair them.

Aircond Not Cold

Why it happensA failed capacitor or outdoor fan motor stops the compressor from running properly. A gas leak or a dirty coil gives the same result.

What we doWe test the capacitor, fan motor and compressor with a meter, then check the gas pressure, so you only pay for the part that failed.

Aircond Not Turning On

Why it happensA blown fuse, a faulty PCB board, a remote or receiver problem, or loose wiring can all stop the unit from starting.

What we doWe check the power supply and wiring first, then the remote signal and PCB, before suggesting any part replacement.

Blinking Light Or Error Code

Why it happensEach blink pattern or code points to a sensor, fan or communication fault between the indoor and outdoor unit.

What we doSend us the code or a photo of the display. We read it against the brand manual and test that part first.

Breaker Trips Or Noisy Unit

Why it happensA shorted compressor, a weak capacitor or a fan blade rubbing the housing causes tripping, buzzing or rattling.

What we doWe find the cause with a meter and a run test, then repair or replace the part and run the unit until it is stable.

How We Repair

We Test The Parts Before We Replace Any

An aircond that stops cooling has usually lost a capacitor or a fan motor, not the compressor most people fear. We check the low-cost parts first and show you what failed.

A blocked drain pipe is the most common cause. Dirt and slime clog the pipe, so water backs up and overflows from the drain pan. Clearing the pipe and cleaning the pan fixes most leaks.
