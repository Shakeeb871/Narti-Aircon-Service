Our Story

A local team with one
simple way of working.

NARTI works out of Taman Pulai Utama in Skudai. We look after wall-mounted and cassette units in homes, condos, shops and small offices across Johor Bahru and the towns around it.

Every job follows the same order. We check the unit, explain the fault in plain words and give you a written price. Nothing gets opened further until you agree to that price.

Aircond Care

How we look after a unit.

From the day it goes on the wall, this is the care that keeps an aircond cooling in the Johor heat.

  1. Day 1

    Installation

    The unit is sized to the room, the piping is vacuumed and the cooling is tested before we hand it over.

  2. 3 mo

    First general service

    Filters, coil and drain get their first clean. Units running most of the day need it at this point.

  3. 6 mo

    Regular general service

    Most homes in Johor settle into a service every three to six months to keep the airflow strong.

  4. 1 yr

    Chemical wash

    The blower wheel and coil come out and soak in coil cleaner to lift the dirt water alone leaves behind.

  5. Any time

    Repair or gas top-up

    Warm air, water leaks or an error code? WhatsApp us and a technician checks the unit on site.
