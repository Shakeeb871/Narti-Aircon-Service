Signs

When your aircond is due for a service

Most homes in Johor need a general service every three to six months. These signs mean it is time now.

  • Air feels weaker than it used to, even on high fan
  • The room takes longer to cool than it did last year
  • A musty or damp smell comes out when the unit starts
  • Black dust spots appear on the louvres or the wall below
  • Water drips from the indoor unit onto the wall or floor
  • The electricity bill has gone up with no change in use

What We Do

Two levels of service for every wall unit

A general service suits a unit that is cleaned regularly. A chemical wash suits a unit that still blows weak air after a general service.

General Service

  • Wash and dry the air filters
  • Clean the evaporator coil and front panel with a water jet
  • Flush the drain pan and drain pipe
  • Clean the outdoor unit fan and condenser coil
  • Measure the air temperature at the louvres before we leave

Chemical Wash

  • Take down the cover, drain pan and blower wheel
  • Soak the coil and blower in coil cleaner to lift built-up dirt and mould
  • Rinse every part and clear the drain pipe
  • Refit the unit and check the fan runs quietly
  • Test the cooling once the unit is back on the wall

FAQs

Aircond Service questions,
answered.

Most units in Johor need a general service every three to six months. A unit that runs most of the day, such as in a bedroom or a shop, needs the shorter interval. A chemical wash about once a year keeps the coil and blower clean.

A general service cleans the filter, coil and drain with water while the unit stays on the wall. A chemical wash takes the blower wheel and drain pan out and soaks the parts in coil cleaner. This removes the dirt that water alone leaves behind.

Clear the space below the indoor unit and move anything that should not get wet. Tell us if the unit has been leaking, smelling or making noise, so we check those parts first.
