Aircond Gas Top-Up In Johor Bahru That Lasts

Aircond gas does not run out on its own. If your unit is low, there is a leak, and topping up without fixing it only buys a few weeks of cooling. We fix the cause first.

01

Leak Found And Fixed First

Low Gas Signs

5 Signs Your Aircond Is Low On Gas

Low gas and a dirty coil can feel the same from the room, so we check the airflow before we connect the gauges.

01

Cool But Never Cold

The air feels cool, not cold, even at 16°C on high fan.

02

Ice On The Copper Pipe

Frost forms on the pipe or indoor coil when the gas level drops.

03

Outdoor Unit Never Stops

The compressor keeps running because it cannot reach the set temperature.

04

Hissing Or Bubbling Sound

A hiss from the piping often points to gas escaping at a joint.

05

Oily Marks On The Joints

Refrigerant oil leaks out with the gas and leaves stains around the pipes.

Why Gas Runs Low

Why Your Aircond Keeps Needing Gas, And How We Fix It

If someone has topped up your aircond gas before and it went warm again, the leak was never fixed. These are the usual causes.

Leaking Flare Joint

Why it happensThe brass nuts joining the pipes to the units loosen or crack over time, and gas escapes slowly.

What we doWe check every joint with leak detector, re-flare or tighten it, then pressure test before adding gas.

Corroded Coil

Why it happensMoisture and dirt eat small holes in the indoor or outdoor coil, especially on older units.

What we doWe locate the leak on the coil and tell you whether it can be repaired or the coil needs replacing, with the price first.

Wrong Gas Or Overfilled

Why it happensMixing gas types or adding too much gas lowers cooling and strains the compressor.

What we doWe top up only the gas shown on your unit label, measured with gauges to the correct running pressure.

Valve Or Service Port Leak

Why it happensWorn valve cores at the outdoor unit let gas seep out after each service.

What we doWe replace the valve core or cap and confirm the pressure holds before we leave.
