Gas Top-Up

Aircond Gas Top-Up in Skudai and Johor Bahru

An aircond that runs all day but blows cool rather than cold air is often low on refrigerant gas.

The gas works in a sealed loop and does not get used up, so a low level means a leak at a joint, a valve or a coil. We find the leak, repair it and top up the correct gas to the pressure the manufacturer sets.

Signs

Signs your aircond is low on gas

Low gas and a dirty coil can feel the same from the room, so we check the airflow before we connect the gauges.

  • The air is cool but never cold, even at 16°C
  • Frost or ice forms on the copper pipe or indoor coil
  • The outdoor unit runs non-stop without cycling off
  • A hissing or bubbling sound comes from the piping
  • Cooling has faded slowly over weeks or months
  • Oily marks show around the pipe joints outside

How We Top Up

Leak first, gas second

Topping up without fixing the leak only buys a few weeks of cooling. We repair the leak so the new gas stays in the system.

Every gas top-up

  • Connect gauges and read the running pressure
  • Check the flare joints, valves and coils for leaks
  • Repair the leak before adding any gas
  • Top up with the gas your unit is built for, such as R32, R410A or R22
  • Measure the pipe and air temperature to confirm the cooling
