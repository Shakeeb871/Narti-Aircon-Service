Aircond Installation

Aircond Installation in Skudai and Johor Bahru

A new aircond cools only as well as it is installed.

Piping left without a vacuum, a drain pipe without enough slope or a unit too small for the room leads to leaks, weak cooling and early compressor failure. We size the unit to the room, fit the piping and drain neatly and test the cooling before we hand it over.

Installations

Installation jobs we take on

From a single bedroom unit to several units in a shop, the same checks apply to every installation.

  • New wall-mounted split units for bedrooms and living rooms
  • Ceiling cassette units for offices and shops
  • Replacing an old unit with a new inverter model
  • Moving an existing unit to another wall or room
  • New copper piping, trunking and drain lines
  • Brackets and platforms for the outdoor unit

What Is Included

What every installation includes

The work behind the wall matters as much as the unit you see, so every installation follows the same steps.

Every installation

  • Site check to match the unit size (HP) to the room
  • Bracket or platform for the outdoor unit
  • Insulated copper piping, drain pipe and electrical wiring
  • Vacuum of the piping before the gas valves open
  • Neat trunking along the wall where the pipes run
  • Full cooling test and a run-through of the remote

FAQs

Aircond Installation questions,
answered.

A room up to about 120 sq ft usually needs 1.0 HP, up to about 180 sq ft needs 1.5 HP, and up to about 250 sq ft needs 2.0 HP. Afternoon sun, ceiling height and the number of people change the size, so we confirm it on site.

Air and moisture left in the pipes mix with the refrigerant and reduce cooling. Over time they damage the compressor. A vacuum pump removes both before we open the gas valves.

Decide which wall the indoor unit goes on and where the outdoor unit will sit. Clear the area around both spots and check there is a free circuit at the DB box.
