FAQs

Aircond Questions
Johor Owners Ask.

Book a service if the unit still cools but smells, drips or blows weak air. Book a repair if it stops cooling, trips the breaker or shows an error code, so our technician can test the failed part.

Service your aircond every three to six months. Bedrooms, shops and offices where the unit runs most of the day need the shorter interval.

A unit that is low on gas runs all day but blows cool rather than cold air, and frost may form on the pipe outside. The gas runs in a sealed loop, so a low level means a leak. We repair the leak before the gas top-up so the new gas stays in.
