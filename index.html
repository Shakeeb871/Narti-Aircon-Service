FAQs

Your questions,
answered.

If the unit still cools but smells or blows weakly, routine servicing is usually enough. When it stops cooling or leaks water, book a repair so the technician can trace the cause on site.

The unit type, its condition and the work it needs. A wall-mounted unit costs less to service than a cassette or ducted system, and a chemical wash costs more than a routine clean. You always see the price before we start.

A unit that is low on gas runs all day but blows cool rather than cold air, and frost may form on the copper pipe. The gas runs in a sealed loop, so a low level means a leak. We repair the leak before the gas top-up so the new gas stays in.

Clear the space below the indoor unit, make sure we can reach the outdoor unit, and tell us about any leaks, smells or noises you have noticed.
