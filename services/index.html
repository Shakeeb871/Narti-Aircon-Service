SERVICES

What We Do

Four services, one team.

Pick the service that matches what your unit is doing. Each one has its own page with the full list of checks.

01

Aircond Service

For a unit that still cools but blows weaker air, smells musty or drips water. We clean the filter, coil, blower and drain so it cools the way it did when new.

  • Wash and dry the air filters
  • Clean the evaporator coil and front panel with a water jet
  • Flush the drain pan and drain pipe
  • Clean the outdoor unit fan and condenser coil
  • Measure the air temperature at the louvres before we leave
View Aircond Service → Book on WhatsApp
02

Aircond Repair

For a unit that stops cooling, leaks, trips the breaker or shows an error code. We test the parts, show you which one failed and give you the price first.

  • Check the power supply, breaker and wiring to both units
  • Test the capacitor, fan motors and compressor with a meter
  • Read the error code and test the sensors and PCB
  • Measure the gas pressure to check for a leak
  • Replace the failed part and run the unit until it cools
View Aircond Repair → Book on WhatsApp
03

Aircond Installation

For a new unit, a replacement or a unit moving to another room. We size it to the room, vacuum the piping and test the cooling before handover.

  • Site check to match the unit size (HP) to the room
  • Bracket or platform for the outdoor unit
  • Insulated copper piping, drain pipe and electrical wiring
  • Vacuum of the piping before the gas valves open
  • Neat trunking along the wall where the pipes run
View Aircond Installation → Book on WhatsApp
04

Gas Top-Up

For a unit that runs all day but never gets cold. We find and repair the leak first, then top up the correct gas to the right pressure.

  • Connect gauges and read the running pressure
  • Check the flare joints, valves and coils for leaks
  • Repair the leak before adding any gas
  • Top up with the gas your unit is built for, such as R32, R410A or R22
  • Measure the pipe and air temperature to confirm the cooling
